Choose one of four views from the top toolbar:

- **Details:** a game list on the left and information about the selected game on the right, with the game's background art.
- **Grid:** covers with the status below each image; selecting a game opens the side panel.
- **List:** one row per game with platform, library, playtime, ratings and achievements.
- **Kanban:** one column per status. Dragging a game to another column changes its status.

Switching views closes the side panel while keeping the selected game highlighted. To play from the couch with a controller, use [fullscreen](fullscreen.md).

## Covers

Every cover in the application, in the library views, the side panel and fullscreen, uses the same upright **3:4** frame, as in Playnite. The art fills the frame: a cover with a different shape loses a little of its edges, with no stretching or blurred filler. Grid cards align at the top; the status strip stays directly below the cover, and long titles and statuses can wrap.

A placeholder appears in the same frame while a cover loads, when no cover is available or if the image fails to load. See [metadata](metadata.md) for where covers come from.

## Search and filter

Search ignores differences in case and accents. **Filters** opens the panel on the right. You can combine statuses, platforms, playtime, ratings and other criteria.

**Favorites** and **Recent** are independent of status. Recent covers the last 30 days. Genre, developer, publisher and release filters depend on available metadata.

## Statuses and ratings

In **Settings → Statuses**, create, rename, reorder or delete statuses. When deleting a status that has games, choose where to move those games. Automatic rules determine the status for newly added games and for their first session.

From a game's **More** menu:

- Add or remove its favorite mark.
- Use **Rate game…** for a star rating and difficulty in peppers, from half to five.
- Use **Write review…** for your own text.
- Choose or remove the background video and the theme music (see below).
- Download metadata or show the ROM in Explorer.

## Background video and theme music

In Details view, a game's background can be a video, played silently on a loop. It comes from the automatic trailer (see [metadata](metadata.md)) or from your own MP4 or WebM, chosen with **More → Choose background video…**; Playtrove keeps its own copy and never touches the original file. With Windows' reduced motion setting, the video starts paused.

Each game can also have **theme music**, played offline on a loop in all four library views. Use **More → Choose theme music…** for your own MP3, M4A or WAV, or let Playtrove find one automatically with the metadata. Playback and volume are in **Settings → General**, and the music pauses when the window loses focus or a game is running.

In version 1.0.0, the view and filters are not remembered when you reopen the application.
